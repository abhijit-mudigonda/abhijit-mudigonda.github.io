I do puzzlehunts with [teammate](https://www.puzzles.wiki/wiki/Teammate) and [Level 1 Spicy](https://www.puzzles.wiki/wiki/Level_1_Spicy).
I was on the writing team for MIT Mystery Hunt 2023 and Teammate Hunt 2025.


There are two puzzles I've written that I'm especially proud of. The first is
[To Numbers And Back Again](https://puzzles.mit.edu/2023/puzzlefactory.place/puzzles/to-numbers-and-back-again/),
which incorporates many different aspects of Indian culture along with many of my other interests.
The second is [Unlocking the Comms Device](https://2025.teammatehunt.com/puzzles/unlocking-the-comms-device) and 
the Captain's office round, for which we constructed a language called Blobbish and integrated it into 
the various puzzles in the round.
